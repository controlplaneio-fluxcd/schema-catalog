spec <object> (required)	# VirtualMachineMove: move one running VM to another Cloud Hypervisor host in its zone, keeping its memory, disk, MAC and addresses (ADR-0127). # What a move guarantees - **The guest runs on exactly one host, always.** Failure before commit leaves it where it was; after commit the old copy is never booted again. - **Admitted only where it can work**: same zone (`banlieue.io/zone`), both hosts advertising `move`, identical VMM version and CPU model, and a destination the VM's own placement allows. Otherwise `Rejected`, with a named reason. - **One move per VM at a time.** Deleting a move before `HandingOver` cancels it; after that it runs to `Succeeded` or `Failed`. # What a move is not Not a restart elsewhere after a host failure: that is fencing and HA, and banlieue does not do it. Until Stage 2 a VM with a vTPM is not movable.
