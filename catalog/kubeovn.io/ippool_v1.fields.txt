spec.ipCount <integer> format=int32 min=1 max=32	# Number of addresses to pick automatically from the subnet's free addresses, per IP family (a dual-stack subnet yields ipCount IPv4 plus ipCount IPv6 addresses). The picked addresses are recorded in status.ips and stay stable; increasing the value appends addresses, decreasing it releases the most recently added ones first. Mutually exclusive with ips. An ippool carves a small set of addresses out of a subnet; needing more than a few dozen is better served by a dedicated subnet, or by spelling out ranges in ips. This bound must stay in sync with maxIPPoolIPCount in the controller, which still guards pools created before the limit was introduced.
