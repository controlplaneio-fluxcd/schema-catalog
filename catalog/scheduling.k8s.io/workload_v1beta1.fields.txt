spec.compositePodGroupTemplates[].podGroupTemplates[].schedulingPolicy.gang.minCount <integer> (required) format=int32	# minCount is the minimum number of pods that must be schedulable or scheduled at the same time for the scheduler to admit the entire group. Updates to minCount may not be immediately reflected in scheduling decisions due to propagation delays. If minCount is updated while a scheduling cycle is in progress for that group, the new value may not take effect until the next cycle.
